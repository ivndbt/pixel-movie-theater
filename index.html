Pixel Movie Theater

Welcome to the Pixel Movie Theater, a pixel club where people share their favorite movies. Inspired by cliques like little pixel library & pixel records pixel club.

Walk down the corridor by scrolling right.
Click a username on a plate to visit that artist’s website.

Join the clique!

  1. Make a pixel art movie poster! It can be your take on a real poster or an original design. Series, webseries & shorts are welcome too.
  2. Posters must be exactly 80x120px. Format allowed are PNG or GIF. Animations are welcome!
  3. Don’t just shrink the original poster down. Take your time & have fun while crafting it pixel by pixel in your own style.
  4. No AI-generated art.
  5. You need your own website to join & a GitHub account to submit your poster.
  6. Ready to join? Open a pull request on the GitHub repo. The README explains how step by step.
  7. If you want to show other people’s posters on your own site: just save a copy, don’t hotlink, & link back to the creator.

If you want to spread this clique or you are taking part of it: save the badge, upload it to your own site & paste the code.
Well done!
